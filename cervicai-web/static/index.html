About CerviAI

This app runs a weighted soft-voting ensemble of three CNN models (ResNet50, DenseNet121, EfficientNet-B3). Each model outputs a probability distribution over five classes. The ensemble computes a weighted sum of probabilities and selects the class with highest fused probability.
